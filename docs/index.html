See who still owes IPPT or NSFIT, and send each person a ready-made WhatsApp reminder. Everything you do is saved straight into your unit's Google Sheet. There is no Save button.

EW1 Before you start

  1. Your Gmail is on the app's user list. Ryan adds it. Without this, Google blocks the sign-in.
  2. Editor access to your unit's Google Sheet. View-only lets you look, but not save reminders or edits.
  3. WhatsApp on your phone, or WhatsApp Desktop on your computer.

Nothing to install. Works in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a phone or a computer.

NE1 Open your sheet

  1. Go to transcom.gyanroh.com. Bookmark it, or add it to your phone's home screen.
  2. Pick your sheet:
    • Browse: tap Browse my Google Drive and choose your sheet. Look under Shared with me if someone else owns it.
    • Paste: copy the sheet's link from Google Sheets, paste it in the box and tap Load from link.
  3. Sign in with the Gmail Ryan added.
  4. Google says "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected for an internal tool: tap Advanced, then Go to TRANSCOM Command Link.
  5. Allow the access Google asks for. The app only opens the sheet you chose, and only its IPPT tab.
Next time: just tap the green Continue button and sign in. Other sheets you've used are listed below it. They are remembered on this device only (sheet names, no personal data). Tap ✕ or Forget to remove one.

Want to look around first? Tap Try with sample data on the start screen. It uses 12 made-up people and saves nothing.

CC1 Fill in your details

The Send buttons stay greyed out until these boxes at the top of Send Reminder are filled:

  • Your name / rank: signs every message, e.g. "Insp Tan".
  • FY: usually filled in for you (FY25 last, FY26 this).
  • Deadline: the month people must finish by, shown in the messages.

These stay on your phone or computer, not in the sheet. Each CO sets their own. Under Edit message wording you can change the messages; Reset to default wording brings back the standard text.

Open chats in: Phone / browser opens WhatsApp on your phone or in the browser; Desktop app opens WhatsApp Desktop.

DT1 Find an officer

The top boxes show how many have cleared IPPT. The list puts people who still owe IPPT first (red line), then all clear (green line).

  • Search by name or HP. Spelling mistakes, a missing "bin" or "s/o" and word order don't matter. A result tagged ~ close is a near match, so check it.
  • Tap a person to open their ticket.
  • Correct a detail by tapping it. Fixed choices (IPPT result, NSFIT track, PES, rank, unit) give a dropdown; pick Type your own… if the value isn't listed. Dates use a date picker.
  • Changes go into the Google Sheet within seconds. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) undoes the last one.

Only fix what you know is wrong. Bigger clean-ups are easier in the Google Sheet itself.

TE1 Send reminders

The app writes each message for you. You still press send in WhatsApp. Use the IPPT sub-tab; filter by unit if you only handle one team.

One at a time: tap Send on a row. WhatsApp opens with the message typed. Send it, then come back: the row shows Sent with the time, and the time is saved in the sheet. Preview shows the message first.

A batch: tap Start batch (up to 20 people not yet messaged). Tap Open chat 1, send in WhatsApp, come back, tap Open chat 2, and so on. Skip leaves someone out; Stop batch ends early.

Wait 1 to 2 days between batches. Messaging many people at once can get your WhatsApp number flagged as spam. The app shows when your next batch can start.

People another CO has already messaged show as Sent and are left out of batches.

NS2 Saving and refreshing

  • All synced: everything is in the sheet.
  • N not synced – Retry: some changes haven't reached the sheet yet (no signal, or sign-in expired). They are kept on your device; tap Retry, or they go through the next time you load or refresh. Don't clear your browser data while this shows.
  • Refresh (in Your Data) reloads the sheet to show what other COs sent or changed. The app also refreshes by itself when you come back after 5 minutes or more.

! Handling personal data

The sheet holds names, phone numbers and emails of real officers.

  • Don't forward the sheet link, screenshots or copies beyond the people who need them.
  • Don't use the app on a shared or public computer. If you must, close the browser when done and tap Forget on the start screen.
  • Data goes only between Google and your device. The app has no server of its own, and nothing is stored on this website.
